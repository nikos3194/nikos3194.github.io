Pixel art location converter

Paste links from Wplace, Youplace, Geopixels, or Bplace (one per line, mixing sites is fine) and get the matching link for the other three.

wplace.live youplace.live geopixels.net bplace.art

Each site's tile/pixel coordinates are derived from latitude and longitude, so the destination might land a handful of pixels off from the original link (a few meters) — enough to get you to the right spot, not necessarily the exact same pixel.

Wplace and Youplace use a Mercator tile grid (zoom 11 and 12 respectively, 1000 units per tile). Geopixels uses real Mercator meters divided by 25, centered at 0,0. Bplace uses Mercator meters divided by 30, with a top-left origin.
